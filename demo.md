**60-second pitch (about 150 words, read aloud at a natural pace)**

**What is happening?**
"Across 750 payments over two months, one system stands out: Gateway B. It misses its SLA on 83% of transactions. CorePay misses on 43% and Gateway A on 45%."

**Why does it matter?**
"Gateway B is slower, with a median of 3.8 seconds against 2.5 for CorePay. It's slow on every one of its five products and on every channel, so this is a system problem, not a product problem. Failure rates are about the same across systems, so customers aren't seeing more errors. They are seeing slower service."

**What should leadership do?**
"Open a performance review of Gateway B, covering capacity and routing."

**What evidence supports that?**
"The dashboard shows it: 83% against 43%, consistent across all products and channels."

**Caveat (5 seconds):**
"This is two months of data, with duplicates removed and 12 missing times excluded. The data doesn't tell us why Gateway B is slow, so the review has to find that out."

**Delivery tips**
- Open with the number: "Gateway B misses its SLA 83% of the time."
- Point to the red bar on the dashboard for the "one visual story" rule.
- If you're running over time, cut the caveat down to one sentence.
